ReferenceClothUtils.cs is copied without changes from that commit. Strict coincident mapping is the default; --allow-nearest explicitly enables the source's general nearest-three heuristics. Those distance heuristics are approximate per the source comments and do not establish in-game correctness.
Supercluster.KDTree.Standard 1.0.5 matches the primary implementation's dependency, with an exact package version/lock and a runtime DLL SHA256 pin.
